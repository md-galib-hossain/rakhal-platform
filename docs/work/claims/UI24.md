## Scope & Implementation Notes

Ensure animal names and tag numbers across the mobile application navigate directly to the target animal profile page (`/cattle/${animalId}`):
1. **Notifications Sheet (`InboxTab` & `UpcomingTab`)**:
   - Detect and resolve associated animal from `item.data`, `item.link` (e.g. `/animals?view=...`, `/cattle/...`), or `cattleList` match in title/body.
   - Render a pressable animal tag chip (`🐮 #TAG · Name`) on notification cards and upcoming cards.
   - Tapping the animal chip or card closes the notification sheet and navigates to `/cattle/${animalId}`.
2. **Milk Module (`MilkCowCard`)**:
   - Make cow avatar, name, and tag header pressable with active touch feedback, navigating directly to `/cattle/${row.cattleId}`.
3. **Cattle Groups (`GroupMembersSheet`)**:
   - Make member rows/names pressable, closing the group sheet and navigating directly to `/cattle/${item.id}`.
4. **Upcoming Events (`UpcomingEventsSheet`)**:
   - Make the animal title header pressable to navigate to `/cattle/${item.id}`.
5. Strict ADR-007 compliance (zero drop shadows; 1px border separation and contrast tokens).
